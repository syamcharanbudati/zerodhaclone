Umesh Yadav is an Indian cricketer and right-arm fast bowler. He was born on **25 October 1987 in Nagpur, Maharashtra**. He started playing professional cricket for Vidarbha and made his international debut for India in 2010. Umesh is known for his **fast bowling, pace, bounce, and ability to swing the ball**. He has represented India in Test, ODI, and T20I cricket and has also played in the IPL for teams such as Kolkata Knight Riders, Royal Challengers Bangalore, and Gujarat Titans. His journey from a modest background to becoming an international cricketer is an inspiration to many young players.
